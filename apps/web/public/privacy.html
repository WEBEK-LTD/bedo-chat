Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Bedo Chat is the private chat service of the Australian Dental Academy ("the Academy", "we", "us") for its students and staff. It is available at bedochat.com and as the Bedo Chat app for Android and iPhone, which show the same service. This policy explains what information Bedo Chat holds, why, who can see it and the choices you have. The Academy is responsible for your information in Bedo Chat.

Information we collect

  • Your phone number. You sign in with it; we send a one-time verification code to it by WhatsApp.
  • Your profile. Your name, and if you add them, a username, a short bio and a profile picture.
  • What you send. Messages, photos, videos, voice and video messages, files, stickers, GIFs, polls and reactions, and the groups and channels you are in.
  • Your settings. Privacy choices, notification choices, folders, muted and pinned chats, and your optional 2-Step Verification password, which is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form that cannot be read back.
  • Device and security information. For each signed-in device: its name and type (browser or app), its internet (IP) address, and when it signed in and was last used. We use this to keep your account secure, to let you see and end your sessions, and to limit abuse.
  • Notification addresses. If you allow notifications, the address your browser, Android or Apple gives us so we can deliver them to that device.
  • Reports and records. Reports you make, and a record of important account and moderation events.

Bedo Chat does not use your location or your contacts, shows no advertising, uses no tracking or analytics services, and never sells your information. The camera and microphone are used only when you choose to take a photo or record a voice or video message.

How we use it

  • To provide the chat service: sign you in, deliver your messages and files, and show your profile to the people you chat with, following your privacy settings.
  • To send you notifications you have allowed.
  • To keep Bedo Chat safe: prevent misuse, review reports and enforce the Academy's rules.
  • To keep the records the Academy needs for its students' safety and its legal obligations.

Who can see your information

  • Other users see what you send in the chats, groups and channels you share with them, and your profile as your privacy settings allow. Your phone number is hidden from other users unless you choose otherwise.
  • Authorised Academy administrators can manage accounts, groups and channels. When a message is reported they see that message only. Access to an account's or chat's full history is limited to senior administrators, needs a stated reason, and every such access is recorded.
  • Service providers that run Bedo Chat for the Academy, only as needed to provide it: Netlify (website hosting) and Supabase (database and file storage), both running in Sydney, Australia; Waabek (sending the WhatsApp verification codes, which receives your phone number and the code); Apple, Google and your browser's push service (delivering notifications); and GIPHY (GIF search, reached through our server so it never sees your device's address).
  • Authorities, if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Bedo Chat is the Academy's record of communication with its students. Messages and files are kept while the service runs, including messages you delete: a deleted message is hidden from everyone, but the Academy keeps a record of it. Verification codes stop working after 5 minutes, sessions end after 30 days without use, and technical records such as connection checks are removed automatically after 30 days.

Deleting your account

In the app or on the website, open Settings → Delete account and send the request. An Academy administrator reviews it and you are told the decision. When it is approved you are signed out on every device, your phone number, name, username, bio and picture are removed from your account, and other users see "Deleted account". Messages you sent stay in the chats and in the Academy's records. You can also ask for deletion by email (below).

Your choices and rights

  • Change your profile and privacy settings at any time in Settings.
  • Turn notifications off in Settings → Notifications or in your device's settings.
  • See and end your signed-in devices in Settings → Devices.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct it, by email.

If you are not satisfied with our answer to a privacy concern, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

Security

All connections to Bedo Chat are encrypted (HTTPS). Files are kept in private storage and opened only through short-lived links given to people who can see them. Sensitive settings are stored encrypted.

Children

Bedo Chat is for the Academy's students and staff. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and tell you in Bedo Chat when the change is important.
