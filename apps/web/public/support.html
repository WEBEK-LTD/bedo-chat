Signing in

  • Enter your phone number with its country code and tap Continue. A 6-digit code arrives on WhatsApp for that number; enter it in Bedo Chat.
  • No code? Check that WhatsApp works on that number, wait a minute, then tap Resend code.
  • If you turned on 2-Step Verification, you are asked for your password after the code.
  • New to Bedo Chat? Signing in for the first time creates your account; you then enter your name.

Notifications

  • Allow notifications when Bedo Chat asks, or tap Turn on at the top of your chat list.
  • Turned them off by mistake? On iPhone: Settings → Bedo Chat → Notifications. On Android: Settings → Apps → Bedo Chat → Notifications. In Bedo Chat: Settings → Notifications.
  • You are not notified about the chat you have open, or about muted chats (except mentions and replies).

Your account

  • Change your name, username, bio and picture in Settings → Edit profile.
  • Choose who sees your phone number, last seen and profile picture, and who can message you, in Settings → Privacy.
  • See and sign out other devices in Settings → Devices.
  • Moved to a new number? Use Settings → Change phone number.
  • To delete your account, open Settings → Delete account. Read what happens in our privacy policy.

Safety

  • To report a message, open its menu and choose Report. The Academy's administrators review every report.
  • To stop someone messaging you, open your chat with them, open its menu and choose Block user.

Requirements

Bedo Chat works on iPhone and iPad (iOS 16 or later), Android phones (Android 7 or later) and in any current web browser at bedochat.com.
